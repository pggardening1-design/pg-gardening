Local knowledge

The bits other people forget to mention

Tree and hedge work in this country is not a free-for-all, and getting it wrong is expensive. Part of the job is knowing what applies before anything gets cut.

  • Protected trees. If a tree has a Tree Preservation Order on it, or stands in a conservation area, work needs council consent or six weeks' written notice. We check with the relevant council first — Blackburn with Darwen, Hyndburn, Ribble Valley, Burnley, Chorley or Preston, depending where you are.
  • Nesting birds. It is a criminal offence under the Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 to damage an active nest. Between roughly March and August we check hedges before cutting, and if there is a nest in there, that section waits.
  • Green waste. Anyone taking waste off your property has to be registered as a waste carrier with the Environment Agency. Ask us for our registration number — and ask anyone else quoting, too.

More about how we work

Storm damage or a tree down?

If a tree or a large limb has come down and is blocking access, sitting on a roof or hanging over a road, ring rather than emailing. Do not go near it if it is anywhere close to a power line — that is a call to the network operator, not to us.

FAQs

Questions we get asked

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes are free and there is no obligation. We come out, look at the job properly, and give you a price for clearly described work.

How soon can you come out?

It depends on the time of year — hedge and grass work gets busy through spring and summer. Storm damage and anything unsafe gets priority. Ring and we will tell you honestly where you would be in the queue.

Do you take the waste away?

Yes, unless you would rather keep the woodchip or logs, which some people do. Anything we take away is handled under our Environment Agency waste carrier registration.

Do you work in winter?

Yes. Winter is actually the best time for a lot of tree work — the leaves are off, you can see the structure of the tree, and the ground damage is easier to manage. Hedge cutting is also better done outside the nesting season.

Can you work if there is no rear access?

Usually. Plenty of gardens around Blackburn have no side gate at all. It changes how the job is run — smaller sections, more carrying, floors sheeted where we come through the house — and that is factored into the quote rather than sprung on you afterwards.

If a tree is near a power line

Do not go near it and do not let anybody else. Ring 105 — it works from anywhere in the UK and connects you to your local electricity network operator, who will attend free of charge. That is not a job for a tree surgeon.

FAQs

Before you get in touch

How quickly will you get back to me?

Usually the same day or the next working day. If it is urgent, ring rather than using the form — we are outside more than we are at a desk.

Do you charge to come and quote?

No. Coming out to look and giving you a price is free, and there is no obligation to go ahead.

Can you quote from photographs?

For small, simple jobs sometimes. For anything involving a tree, no — a photograph does not show what is behind it, what is underneath it, or how the waste gets out. We would rather see it than give you a number we have to change later.

Do you take card payments?

Ask when we quote and we will tell you what we can take. Owner: confirm which payment methods you accept and this answer gets updated.

Prefer to look first?

Have a look at the before and after photos from real jobs, then decide.

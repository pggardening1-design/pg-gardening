Not sure which council you are under?

Neither are a lot of people, particularly along the boundaries around Wilpshire, Mellor Brook and Rishton. We check it against your actual address when we quote, so you do not have to work it out.

Outside the list

If your town is not here

The list above is where we work regularly. It is not a fence. We will travel further for bigger planned jobs — a full day of tree work or a clearance — where it makes sense for both of us.

What we will not do is take on a weekly grass cut an hour away and then let you down when the diary gets tight in June. If we cannot look after you properly, we will say so on the phone.
